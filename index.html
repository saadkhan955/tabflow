How It Works

Three simple steps to declutter your browser and save your watch queue.

1

Open Video Tabs

Browse YouTube as you normally do across multiple tabs and browser windows.

2

Open TabFlow

Click the TabFlow toolbar icon to see all detected YouTube tabs with video titles, thumbnails, and duplicate badges.

3

Save & Free RAM

Select your target playlist and save your videos. TabFlow can close the saved tabs automatically to free up memory.
