TabFlow for YouTube

An open-source browser extension designed to help YouTube power users, researchers, and students organize open video tabs, create playlists in 1 click, purge duplicate videos, and auto-categorize libraries.

Application Purpose & Overview

Having dozens of open YouTube video tabs slows down browser performance and makes managing watch queues difficult. TabFlow for YouTube solves this problem by providing a simple, secure, and private browser extension that connects directly to the official YouTube Data API.

Key purposes of TabFlow for YouTube include:

  • Batch Tab Management: Automatically detects active YouTube tabs across all browser windows and enables users to save them directly into any YouTube playlist.
  • RAM & Resource Optimization: Frees system memory by allowing users to close saved video tabs once they are securely stored in a playlist.
  • Duplicate Video Cleaner: Scans individual playlists or your entire YouTube channel to locate and eliminate redundant duplicate video entries.
  • Smart Genre Categorization: Automatically clusters videos into Anime, Tech, Music, Gaming, and Education playlists using lightweight topic and metadata classification.
  • Zero Tracking & Local Processing: All video extraction and settings are processed 100% locally on your machine with direct, encrypted communication to Google's official API servers.

Core Features

1-Click Tab Saving

Scans open YouTube video tabs across single or multiple windows and saves selected videos into any playlist in seconds.

Channel Duplicate Cleaner

Identifies duplicate video entries across all channel playlists and resolves them with 1-click batch cleanup.

Smart AI Genres

Organizes videos into dedicated playlists for Anime & Manga, Tech, Music, Education, and Podcasts.

Instant Watch Queue

Launches an instant YouTube continuous queue with all your open tabs without requiring login.

How It Works

1
Open YouTube Tabs: Browse YouTube as you normally do across multiple tabs and windows.
2
Open TabFlow: Click the TabFlow icon in your Chrome toolbar to instantly view all detected video titles, thumbnails, and duplicate indicators.
3
Save to Playlist: Select your target playlist (or create a new one) and click "Save Selected Videos".
