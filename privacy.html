Privacy Policy for YouTube Tabs Saver

Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Last Updated: September 1, 2026

Summary: YouTube Tabs Saver operates locally in your browser. We do NOT collect, sell, monetize, or transmit your personal data, browser history, or YouTube credentials to external third-party servers. All interactions with the YouTube API occur directly between your browser and Google's official API servers.

1. Information We Access and How It Is Used

  • Open Browser Tabs (tabs permission): Used exclusively to detect open YouTube video tabs (matching youtube.com/watch, /shorts/, /live/, or youtu.be) to extract video IDs and titles for saving. Tab URLs are never logged or transmitted anywhere.
  • YouTube Data API v3 (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube): Used exclusively at your direct request to:
    • Fetch your own playlists to display in the selector.
    • Insert selected videos into your designated playlist.
    • Identify and remove duplicate entries from your playlists.
    • Fetch video metadata (categories and topics) for genre organization.
  • Local Storage (storage permission): Used to store your UI preferences (such as auto-close tabs preference) and temporary cached playlist lists directly on your device.

2. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

YouTube Tabs Saver's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • We do NOT use Google user data for serving advertisements.
  • We do NOT allow humans to read user data.
  • We do NOT transfer or sell user data to any third party.

3. Data Retention and Security

Your OAuth tokens are managed securely by Chrome's built-in chrome.identity API and stored in your browser's private extension storage. You can disconnect your YouTube account and clear all local data at any time by clicking "Sign Out" in the extension settings or removing the extension from Chrome.

4. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service accessed is Google LLC (YouTube Data API v3 and Google OAuth2). Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for information on how Google manages your data.
